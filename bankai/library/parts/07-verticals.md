# Vertical excellence — best-in-class by industry

The reference product of each vertical — the sites and apps designers point to when they say "craft".
Each entry names the specific mechanism worth stealing, not just the brand. Web and mobile, ten industries.

## Automotive

### Porsche Car Configurator — https://configurator.porsche.com
Platform: Web · Vertical: automotive
- **Mechanism**: Red Dot-winning photoreal 3D configurator where nearly every option is visually represented (billions of combinations), with free camera perspective on the car and 3D detail animations instead of option checkboxes
- **Mechanism 2**: 360° interactive mode with openable doors, trunk and frunk; a companion AR Visualizer app drops your exact spec into your driveway
- **Steal for**: make every configuration decision visual, never a text list

### Rimac Configurator — https://www.rimac-automobili.com
Platform: Web · Vertical: automotive
- **Mechanism**: Real-time rendered configurator built in Unreal Engine (with NVIDIA) — paint, cabin and material changes render instantly with no pre-baked image layers per option
- **Mechanism 2**: Nevera R configurator (2025) scales the same real-time pipeline to hundreds of bespoke luxury options in an immersive commissioning flow
- **Steal for**: live material swapping instead of swapped-out photography

### Polestar — https://www.polestar.com
Platform: Web · Vertical: automotive
- **Mechanism**: Scroll-driven product narrative (built with Code & Theory) — the page unfolds as a showroom walkthrough where each scroll beat reveals one spec story, typography and palette as restrained as the car
- **Mechanism 2**: "Outside the showroom glass" UX: marketing site and configurator share one continuous design system instead of separate silos
- **Steal for**: the scroll itself as the product tour

## Luxury fashion

### Dior — https://www.dior.com
Platform: Web · Vertical: luxury
- **Mechanism**: Digital flagship where couture campaign films and savoir-faire craft editorial sit one click away from checkout — storytelling and commerce in the same navigation fabric
- **Mechanism 2**: Collection-event microsites with their own art direction per drop, treated like magazine issues
- **Steal for**: editorial-to-cart continuity without breaking the spell

### Balenciaga — https://www.balenciaga.com
Platform: Web · Vertical: luxury
- **Mechanism**: Deliberate anti-luxury brutalism — wireframe-like grids, plain monospaced-feel type, monochrome chrome — widely cited as the fashion reference for raw minimalism
- **Mechanism 2**: Campaign slogans used as interface copy ("No blabla", "Probably not what you're looking for")
- **Steal for**: restraint as a brand flex; UI so quiet the product carries the page

### Jacquemus — https://www.jacquemus.com
Platform: Web · Vertical: luxury
- **Mechanism**: Surreal 3D-rendered campaign imagery woven directly into e-commerce, so product pages read as lookbook spreads (Ecommerce Design Awards winner)
- **Mechanism 2**: Playful physical-digital stunts (giant-product visuals, immersive "Le Bleu" scenography with Random Studio) that feed the site's visual language
- **Steal for**: the campaign image as the primary interface element

### Gucci App — https://www.gucci.com
Platform: iOS/Android · Vertical: luxury
- **Mechanism**: First-mover AR try-on in fashion: Ace sneakers on your own feet in-app (2019), later watches on your wrist
- **Mechanism 2**: Sneaker Garage with avatar dressing and the digital-only Virtual 25 sneaker — a product that exists purely in AR
- **Steal for**: playful AR as a retention toy, not a gimmick

## Media & streaming

### Netflix — https://www.netflix.com
Platform: Both · Vertical: media
- **Mechanism**: Row-and-boxart browse canvas — the grid is a merchandising surface with hover-to-autoplay previews and resume/progress bars baked into the artwork itself
- **Mechanism 2**: Personalized row titles and social-proof rows (Top 10 in your country) that re-merchandise the same catalog per user
- **Steal for**: navigation as a curation canvas

### MUBI — https://mubi.com
Platform: Both · Vertical: media
- **Mechanism**: One-film-a-day scarcity model — a film arrives, stays 30 days, leaves; the film-of-the-day slot turns the whole service into an appointment
- **Mechanism 2**: Notebook editorial integrated into the browsing surface, so reading and watching are one flow
- **Steal for**: scarcity as interface

### Criterion Channel — https://www.criterionchannel.com
Platform: Both · Vertical: media
- **Mechanism**: Programming-as-product: double features, filmmaker retrospectives, and weekly themed salons replace the infinite anonymous grid
- **Steal for**: curated bundles over algorithmic rows

### Letterboxd — https://letterboxd.com
Platform: Both · Vertical: media
- **Mechanism**: Diary + half-star ratings + one-line review culture built around a poster grid; your watchlist and diary become an identity object
- **Steal for**: logging as the social loop, not sharing

## Travel & hospitality

### Airbnb — https://www.airbnb.com
Platform: Both · Vertical: travel
- **Mechanism**: 2022 category redesign ("I'm flexible" → Airbnb Categories) — inspire-first browsing by vibe (cabin, tiny home, vineyard) instead of query-first search
- **Mechanism 2**: Split-view search results with map and listing list moving together; destination pages with pinned-map storytelling
- **Steal for**: a browsing mode for people who don't know where they're going

### Hopper — https://www.hopper.com
Platform: iOS/Android · Vertical: travel
- **Mechanism**: Price prediction as UI: color-coded fare calendar plus a plain-language "buy now or wait" recommendation with confidence level
- **Mechanism 2**: Watch-a-fare push alerts and fintech add-ons like price freeze — uncertainty turned into a product you can hold
- **Steal for**: visualizing uncertainty as advice

### Trainline — https://www.thetrainline.com
Platform: Both · Vertical: travel
- **Mechanism**: Post-booking companion pattern — live train times, platform changes and disruption alerts make the app useful every journey day, not just purchase day
- **Mechanism 2**: Home-screen "next train" widget and automatic split-ticket savings applied for you
- **Steal for**: owning the day of travel, not just the transaction

## Food

### Wolt — https://wolt.com
Platform: Both · Vertical: food
- **Mechanism**: Motion-rich order tracking where the delivery timer hides mini-games, and scale transitions zoom from storefront into the courier map
- **Mechanism 2**: A house illustration system and 2026 redesign that systematized rounded forms and adaptive motion across the whole app
- **Steal for**: delight engineered into the waiting state

### Domino's Pizza Tracker — https://www.dominos.com
Platform: Both · Vertical: food
- **Mechanism**: The canonical order-status ritual: staged progress (placed → making → quality check → delivering, now simplified to placed/make/deliver) with named store crew — 1.8B+ orders tracked since 2007
- **Steal for**: progress theater that kills "where is my order" anxiety

### Too Good To Go — https://toogoodtogo.com
Platform: iOS/Android · Vertical: food
- **Mechanism**: Surprise-bag mechanic — mystery contents at a fixed low price make surplus inventory feel like a treasure hunt rather than leftovers
- **Mechanism 2**: Personal meals-saved counter turning individual purchases into cumulative impact
- **Steal for**: gamifying imperfect inventory

### Resy — https://resy.com
Platform: Both · Vertical: food
- **Mechanism**: Table-watch notifications for sold-out time slots — scarcity handled with a waitlist instead of a dead end
- **Mechanism 2**: Editorial curation of "the 8pm experience" (occasion-based discovery) layered over map browsing
- **Steal for**: notification-based booking for offline inventory

## Sports

### Nike Run Club — https://www.nike.com/nrc-app
Platform: iOS/Android · Vertical: sports
- **Mechanism**: Audio-guided runs: coach narration ducked under your own music with in-run audio milestone cues — an interface that lives in your ears, not on screen
- **Steal for**: eyes-free UX for a moving user

### Nike SNKRS — https://www.nike.com/snkrs-app
Platform: iOS/Android · Vertical: sports
- **Mechanism**: Hype-commerce choreography: draws (raffle entries), FCFS drops, and Exclusive Access tiers for limited releases
- **Mechanism 2**: Location-based AR "Stashes" that unlock hidden purchase windows, plus "Behind the Design" storytelling per drop
- **Steal for**: designing the launch-day event, not just the product page

### Strava — https://www.strava.com
Platform: Both · Vertical: sports
- **Mechanism**: Segments — any stretch of road becomes a permanent leaderboard, converting solo workouts into asynchronous competition
- **Mechanism 2**: Global Heatmap built from trillions of data points, doubling as the best route-discovery map in sports
- **Steal for**: user-generated competitive surfaces

### F1 Live Timing — https://www.formula1.com/en/timing/f1-live
Platform: Both · Vertical: sports
- **Mechanism**: Second-screen telemetry: bird's-eye driver tracker map, tyre-stint history, sector times and gaps in a live leaderboard synced to the race broadcast
- **Steal for**: pro-grade data made broadcast-companionable

## Health

### Whoop — https://www.whoop.com
Platform: iOS/Android · Vertical: health
- **Mechanism**: One number to act on — a single Recovery score (0–100) headlines the day, and the Strain Target ring converts it into today's effort prescription
- **Mechanism 2**: Layered drilldown — score → contributing metrics → raw signal — so dense biometrics compress into three readable depths
- **Steal for**: one headline metric that prescribes behavior

### Oura — https://ouraring.com
Platform: iOS/Android · Vertical: health
- **Mechanism**: Readiness/Sleep/Activity triad as the app's information architecture; 2025 redesign signals body-state through a color system for instant visual reads
- **Mechanism 2**: Health Radar view consolidating cardiovascular, respiratory and strain signals into one radar glance
- **Steal for**: color as physiological status

### Headspace — https://www.headspace.com
Platform: Both · Vertical: health
- **Mechanism**: Animation-led UX (with Nexus Studios): mascot illustrations and breathing-paced motion do the calming before the content starts; expanded into a full custom-typeface brand system in the 2024 rebrand
- **Steal for**: motion carrying the therapeutic job

### Clue — https://helloclue.com
Platform: iOS/Android · Vertical: health
- **Mechanism**: Data-first cycle tracking designed against category cliché — no pink florals; a neutral cycle calendar with layered symptom logging and prediction
- **Steal for**: winning a category by refusing its visual conventions

## Music

### Spotify — https://www.spotify.com
Platform: Both · Vertical: music
- **Mechanism**: Canvas — 3–8 second vertical artist-shot loops replacing static album art in the Now Playing view, turning the player into artist real estate
- **Mechanism 2**: Wrapped — the annual story-format data ritual that converts listening history into shareable identity cards
- **Steal for**: playback screens as artist canvas and data as celebration

### SoundCloud — https://soundcloud.com
Platform: Both · Vertical: music
- **Mechanism**: Timed waveform comments — annotations pinned to exact moments in the track; the waveform doubles as scrubber, progress bar and comment canvas (so influential YouTube copied it)
- **Steal for**: conversation anchored in media time

### Bandcamp — https://bandcamp.com
Platform: Both · Vertical: music
- **Mechanism**: Artist-as-storefront: full-album streaming above a buy rail, name-your-price purchases, and a fan feed showing who bought what
- **Steal for**: commerce mechanics that feel like patronage

## Developer tools

### Raycast — https://www.raycast.com
Platform: macOS · Vertical: developer tools
- **Mechanism**: One input surface for everything — a root command palette extended by 1000+ community extensions, snippets, clipboard history and AI commands living in the same HUD
- **Steal for**: everything addressable from a single launcher

### Linear — https://linear.app
Platform: Web · Vertical: developer tools
- **Mechanism**: Keyboard-first triage — Cmd/Ctrl+K everywhere, instant local-feeling views, and cycles/timelines that layer project detail progressively
- **Steal for**: raw speed treated as the design feature

### Arc — https://arc.net
Platform: macOS · Vertical: developer tools
- **Mechanism**: Spaces — color-coded contexts (work/personal) living as swappable sections of one sidebar; plus Split View panes and a Command Bar (⌘T) replacing the URL bar
- **Mechanism 2**: Boosts — per-site custom CSS/Safari extensions letting users redesign any website; development froze in 2025, but it remains the canonical browser reimagining study
- **Steal for**: spatial context switching instead of window chaos

### Vercel — https://vercel.com
Platform: Web · Vertical: developer tools
- **Mechanism**: A preview deployment per git branch with an immutable URL, plus commenting directly on the live preview — review happens on the real artifact, not screenshots
- **Steal for**: making the review surface the product itself

## AI products

### Perplexity — https://www.perplexity.ai
Platform: Both · Vertical: AI
- **Mechanism**: Trust as layout — structured answer cards with inline numbered citations, source chips per claim, and related follow-up queries that steer the next step
- **Steal for**: verifiability built into the answer's visual structure

### Cursor — https://cursor.com
Platform: Desktop · Vertical: AI
- **Mechanism**: Tab — a completion model that predicts your next edit (not just characters) from recent edits, surrounding context and lint errors, shown as ghost text
- **Mechanism 2**: Cmd+K inline text-to-diff editing and agent changes presented as reviewable diffs, so AI output is always a first-class editor citizen
- **Steal for**: AI suggestions that respect the editing surface

### Claude Artifacts — https://claude.ai
Platform: Web · Vertical: AI
- **Mechanism**: Side panel (~40% of viewport) that renders runnable code, docs and pages live beside the chat — chat stays the control surface, the panel is the product
- **Mechanism 2**: Preview/Code mode toggle and version navigation for iterating on generated output
- **Steal for**: conversation as command line, artifact as the app

### NotebookLM — https://notebooklm.google.com
Platform: Web · Vertical: AI
- **Mechanism**: Strictly source-grounded notebook — answers cite only the documents you uploaded, making grounding itself the feature
- **Mechanism 2**: One-click Audio Overview that turns your sources into a two-host podcast dialogue
- **Steal for**: scoped knowledge as a trust mechanic and format-shifting as output

## Study these first

1. **Porsche Car Configurator** — the ceiling for visual configuration UX
2. **Airbnb** — the reference for inspiration-first browsing and category design
3. **Spotify** — the best case for playback surfaces as artist real estate and data as celebration
4. **Whoop** — the masterclass in compressing dense data into one actionable number
5. **Linear** — the proof that speed and keyboard fluency are design features
