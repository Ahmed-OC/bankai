# Global — the non-English design world

Japan is known for craft-grade restraint, gallery/credit culture and heritage brands with modern sites; Korea for maximalist festival campaign sites, mega-portals and its own award guilds; China for viral H5 campaign pages and super-app density. France and Germany run award-luxury motion studios, the Nordics ship humanistic functionalism, Russia does civic-scale expression, and Spain/Mexico bring editorial and LatAm interactive storytelling — none of it looks like the English Awwwards mainstream.

## Japan

### Web Design Clip (ウェブデザインクリップ) — https://webdesignclip.com
Platform: Web · Origin: Japan · Type: media/gallery
- **Mechanism**: 6,400+ Japanese sites filterable by 業種 (industry), 表現 (expression technique) and 技術 (tech used), each entry credited to its production company — a searchable taxonomy of *how* a Japanese site is built, not just what it looks like
- **Steal for**: researching Japanese production patterns by technique, and finding the studios behind them

### MUUUUU.ORG (ムウウウ) — https://muuuuu.org
Platform: Web · Origin: Japan · Type: media/gallery
- **Mechanism**: color-palette-first filtering — pick a palette and get every Japanese site built on it, plus a daily Site of the Day with full creator credits
- **Steal for**: palette-driven moodboarding and daily scans of Japan's best new work

### SANKOU! (参考デザイン) — https://sankoudesign.com
Platform: Web · Origin: Japan · Type: media/gallery
- **Mechanism**: categories mapped to real Japanese business deliverables — landing pages, 資料請求 (document request), 採用 (recruiting), event/festival — with maker credits on every clip
- **Steal for**: seeing how Japanese studios solve conversion pages and recruiting sites, genres the West barely designs for

### HOSOO (細尾) — https://www.hosoo.co.jp
Platform: Web · Origin: Japan · Type: brand (heritage craft)
- **Mechanism**: a 1688 Nishijin textile house whose site treats extreme macro photography of woven threads as full-bleed brand texture, with quiet bilingual typography — material close-up as the entire art direction (Milano Design Award winner)
- **Steal for**: heritage brands — let one material image carry the whole site

### KAIKADO (開化堂) — https://www.kaikado.jp
Platform: Web · Origin: Japan · Type: brand (heritage craft)
- **Mechanism**: Kyoto's oldest tea-caddy maker (1875) with an ultra-minimal product-as-hero site that narrates *patina* — copper and tin aging over years — as the product story
- **Steal for**: one-object storytelling where time itself is the feature

### DEPT (株式会社デパート) — https://depart-inc.com
Platform: Web · Origin: Japan · Type: studio
- **Mechanism**: Japanese creative company whose 10th-anniversary microsite took an Awwwards Honorable Mention (2025) — the Japanese corporate ritual of the 記念 (anniversary) site as a portfolio spectacle
- **Steal for**: turning company milestones into award-grade campaign sites

### SHIFTBRAIN (株式会社シフトブレイン) — https://shiftbrain.com
Platform: Web · Origin: Japan · Type: studio
- **Mechanism**: branding studio whose `/zeroz` brand site won the Awwwards + FWA + CSS Design Awards triple crown; their own site runs scroll-choreographed kinetic typography and they publish a Standard Design Unit sub-site on design standards
- **Steal for**: velocity-driven type motion and the "brand site as product" doctrine

### Garden Eight (ガーデンエイト) — https://garden-eight.com
Platform: Web · Origin: Japan · Type: studio
- **Mechanism**: top of Awwwards' Japan leaderboard; Tokyo (Shimokitazawa) with a Copenhagen foot — playful-but-humble WebGL scenes instead of tech-demo aggression
- **Steal for**: 3D that serves charm, not spectacle

## Korea

### GDWEB (지디웹) — https://www.gdweb.co.kr
Platform: Web · Origin: Korea · Type: media/award gallery
- **Mechanism**: Korea's main web-design award + benchmarking gallery, judged by industry category (education, public, culture/art, lifestyle) — the reference for what Korean clients consider "good"
- **Steal for**: tracking Korean award taste and industry-specific benchmarks

### Web Award Korea (웹어워드 코리아) — https://www.i-award.or.kr
Platform: Web · Origin: Korea · Type: award/festival
- **Mechanism**: 4,000 internet professionals peer-jury the year's sites and publish trend forecasts for Korean web — an industry award run like a festival
- **Steal for**: seeing the selection criteria of an entire national web industry

### WATERBOMB Festival (워터밤 페스티벌) — https://www.waterbombfestival.com
Platform: Web/Mobile · Origin: Korea · Type: festival
- **Mechanism**: campaign-poster energy as a full site — slogan typography ("SEOUL GETS WET AGAIN"), lineup-as-poster pages, ticket-first information architecture, and per-city franchise microsites (e.g., waterbombhochiminh.com) sharing one brand system
- **Steal for**: event sites that feel like merch, and franchise-scale brand consistency

### Incheon Pentaport Rock Festival (인천 펜타포트 락 페스티벌) — https://pentaport.co.kr
Platform: Web/Mobile · Origin: Korea · Type: festival
- **Mechanism**: grunge/editorial art direction where the site texture (rough type, zine layout) is coded to the festival's rock genre — genre-matched visual language, not generic festival templates
- **Steal for**: art direction that literally sounds like the music

### Naver (네이버) — https://www.naver.com
Platform: Web/Mobile · Origin: Korea · Type: portal
- **Mechanism**: Korea's mega-portal proves high information density can be beautiful — a strictly rhythmic module grid unifying search, news, shopping and dozens of services, the polar opposite of Western minimalism
- **Steal for**: dense dashboard/portal layouts that stay calm through module rhythm

### Public Design Festival (공공디자인 페스티벌) — https://festival.publicdesign.kr
Platform: Web/Mobile · Origin: Korea · Type: festival/public sector
- **Mechanism**: government-run design festival site with a nationwide public-design node map — civic data visualization as a festival identity
- **Steal for**: public-sector sites with map-driven storytelling

## China

### ZCOOL (站酷) — https://www.zcool.com.cn
Platform: Web/Mobile · Origin: China · Type: media/community
- **Mechanism**: China's largest designer community, including deep case teardowns of viral work (e.g., the analysis of why the Taobao Maker Festival H5 exploded) — Chinese design discourse in the raw
- **Steal for**: how Chinese designers deconstruct their own viral hits

### UIGREAT — https://uigreat.com
Platform: Web · Origin: China · Type: media/gallery
- **Mechanism**: inspiration gallery curated to Chinese product conventions — dense information layouts, red-accent branding, super-app UI patterns rarely seen in Western galleries
- **Steal for**: Chinese-market UI patterns before localizing a product

### Tencent ISUX (腾讯ISUX用户体验设计) — https://isux.tencent.com
Platform: Web · Origin: China · Type: media (in-house design team)
- **Mechanism**: Tencent's user-experience department publishes WeChat/QQ design case studies in Chinese — the design-system thinking behind billion-user super-apps
- **Steal for**: first-party design rationale for products at population scale

### NetEase Cloud Music annual report (网易云音乐年度听歌报告) — https://music.163.com
Platform: Mobile · Origin: China · Type: app/campaign H5
- **Mechanism**: the original "Wrapped"-style H5 that floods WeChat Moments every December — seasonal scene-based color (grass/sea/rice-field/snow), DIY avatar, 3D animation, and a red "share to friend" button as the final CTA with deliberately non-judgmental content so sharing is ego-safe
- **Steal for**: personal-data storytelling engineered end-to-end for social forwarding

### H5Anli (H5案例分享) — https://www.h5anli.com
Platform: Web · Origin: China · Type: media/case library
- **Mechanism**: the archive of the Chinese H5 canon, including milestone pieces like the Taobao Maker Festival invitation (panoramic comic, one-take scroll that defined the panoramic-H5 genre) — each case documented with interaction breakdowns
- **Steal for**: the full history of scroll-driven mobile campaign craft

### Digitaling (数英) — https://www.digitaling.com
Platform: Web · Origin: China · Type: media/case database
- **Mechanism**: China's digital-marketing case database with "why it went viral" teardowns — the commercial layer behind Chinese creative work
- **Steal for**: campaign strategy attached to the visuals

### Bakken & Bæck — https://bakkenbaeck.com
