# Mobile — iOS design-award apps

The PRIORITY zone of the library: apps Apple itself and the design community crowned for craft.
Every entry names concrete, stealable UI/UX mechanisms — interactions, not vibes — verified to exist.
Use these as the ceiling for what mobile interface polish means: input, glanceability, gesture, haptics, personality.

## Productivity, notes & writing

### Things 3 — https://culturedcode.com/things/
Platform: iOS · Domain: productivity / to-do · Recognition: Apple Design Award 2017 winner (two-time ADA winner)
- **Mechanism**: the "Magic Plus" button — a draggable floating action button. Grab it and the app blooms open with drop targets (Inbox, Today, projects, headings); drop it exactly where the new to-do should land, with spring physics on every target.
- **Mechanism 2**: signature completion micro-interaction — tapping a checkbox draws the checkmark stroke, then the item slides away into the Logbook with a bounce; the "When" date picker is a thumb-scroll scroller with This Evening / Someday shortcut rows.
- **Steal for**: any list UI and quick-capture flow.

### Bear — https://bear.app
Platform: iOS · Domain: notes / writing · Recognition: Apple Design Award 2017 winner; App Store App of the Year 2016
- **Mechanism**: nested hashtags as the filing system — type `#` and an inline autocomplete offers `home/rent`-style hierarchies; tags are live links that double as filters, so there is no folder-management mode at all.
- **Mechanism 2**: live Markdown rendering while typing (asterisks become bold as you type, syntax characters disappear) plus typography-first themes and a custom typeface tuned for long-form reading.
- **Steal for**: editor surfaces and tagging models.

### Agenda — https://agenda.com
Platform: iOS · Domain: notes / productivity · Recognition: Apple Design Award 2018 winner
- **Mechanism**: notes pinned to a calendar timeline — every note carries a date; the timeline scrubber is the primary navigation, with past notes dimming and "completing" themselves instead of being deleted.
- **Mechanism 2**: NOW / SOON buckets plus color-coded category rails that organize by urgency before chronology.
- **Steal for**: time-anchored organization of non-calendar content.

### Craft — https://www.craft.do
Platform: iOS · Domain: docs / notes · Recognition: App Store Awards 2021 — Mac App of the Year
- **Mechanism**: fluid block hierarchy — any block (line, image, todo) can be demoted into a sub-page, and any sub-page can be merged back into flowing text; hierarchy is a gesture, not a commitment.
- **Mechanism 2**: daily-note card stack, swipe-based creation/editing, and a slash-command palette for styling without toolbars.
- **Steal for**: block editors and document authoring UX.

### Fantastical — https://flexibits.com/fantastical
Platform: iOS · Domain: calendar · Recognition: App Store Editors' Choice, long-running best-of lists
- **Mechanism**: natural-language event creation with live token highlighting — type "Lunch with Ana Friday 12:30 at Sweetgreen" and watch date, time, place and invitee get parsed and colored inline as you type.
- **Mechanism 2**: the DayTicker — a horizontally scrolling day ribbon docked directly above the calendar, always thumb-reachable; Calendar Sets auto-switch by time of day or location.
- **Steal for**: any creation form — parse instead of fields.

### iA Writer — https://ia.net/writer
Platform: iOS · Domain: writing · Recognition: Apple Design Awards 2025 finalist (Interaction)
- **Mechanism**: Focus Mode — everything dims except the sentence you're currently editing; reading rhythm beats scrolling.
- **Mechanism 2**: Syntax Control — nouns, verbs, adjectives and conjunctions tinted different colors so prose structure becomes editable visually; swipe-based navigation between library, editor and preview.
- **Steal for**: calm, distraction-free writing surfaces.

### Play — https://createwithplay.com
Platform: iOS · Domain: design tool / prototyping · Recognition: Apple Design Awards 2025 winner (Innovation); acquired by Apple in 2026 and wound down
- **Mechanism**: prototyping with real SwiftUI — screens are built from actual native UI materials, so prototypes feel like the shipped app because they compile to real SwiftUI code for handoff.
- **Mechanism 2**: design on iPhone/Mac with real-time collaboration and sync — the prototype and its code exist simultaneously.
- **Steal for**: design-to-code workflows and prototyping tools.

### Crouton — https://apps.apple.com/us/app/crouton-recipe-manager/id1461650987
Platform: iOS · Domain: cooking / recipes · Recognition: Apple Design Awards 2024 winner (Interaction)
- **Mechanism**: cooking mode — one step at a time in large type, with a wink (blink left or right, detected by the camera) to advance steps hands-free when your hands are messy.
- **Mechanism 2**: ingredients flow into a grocery list synced with Apple Reminders, where checked items auto-clear after a short delay; per-step timers start with a single tap.
- **Steal for**: step-through flows — recipes, workouts, setup instructions.

## Weather

### CARROT Weather — https://www.carrotweather.com
Platform: iOS · Domain: weather · Recognition: Apple Design Awards 2021 winner (Interaction)
- **Mechanism**: Interface builder — a drag-to-reorder, tap-to-hide card system that lets every user compose a personal weather dashboard; later Smart Layout re-orders cards automatically based on conditions.
- **Mechanism 2**: personality as interface — a snarky AI character delivers forecasts, with achievements and secret scenes rewarding exploration; data becomes entertainment without losing accuracy.
- **Steal for**: customizable dashboards and settings-with-a-voice.

## Photo, video & creative tools

### Halide Mark II — https://halide.cam
Platform: iOS · Domain: camera / photography · Recognition: Apple Design Awards 2022 winner (Visuals and Graphics)
- **Mechanism**: dial-metaphor gestures — vertical swipe adjusts exposure, horizontal swipe adjusts focus, all one-thumbed, no mode menus; manual controls (ISO, shutter, WB) bloom on demand and recede.
- **Mechanism 2**: progressive disclosure around hardware — UI adapts around the notch/Dynamic Island; zero-processing RAW capture for those who want it, smart auto for everyone else.
- **Steal for**: pro tools that stay one-handed — progressive disclosure done right.

### Kino — https://www.shotwithkino.com
Platform: iOS · Domain: video camera · Recognition: App Store Awards 2024 — iPhone App of the Year
- **Mechanism**: instant grade — you shoot through expert-made color presets (LUTs) with a live preview, so cinematic output requires no editing pass; import your own LUTs.
- **Mechanism 2**: a minimal record surface hiding pro toggles (Apple Log capture, frame controls) that only appear when summoned.
- **Steal for**: preview-before-commit patterns — show the outcome before the user captures.

### Procreate — https://procreate.com
Platform: iOS (iPad) · Domain: drawing / painting · Recognition: Apple Design Awards 2022 winner (Inclusivity)
- **Mechanism**: QuickMenu — a fully customizable radial palette summoned by a touch gesture (tap-and-hold); press-and-hold any button to swap in the function you want, no settings trip.
- **Mechanism 2**: precision sliders — touch the brush-size slider then keep dragging sideways anywhere on canvas for fine control; Quick Pinch snaps the canvas to fit instantly.
- **Steal for**: dense tool UIs and gesture shortcut systems.

### Procreate Dreams — https://procreate.com/dreams
Platform: iOS (iPad) · Domain: animation / video · Recognition: Apple Design Awards 2024 winner (Innovation)
- **Mechanism**: direct-manipulation timeline — clips and keyframes are grabbed, stretched, flung and flipped with fingers and Pencil; playback keeps running while you edit, so timing is judged live.
- **Mechanism 2**: performative effects — motions and transitions respond like instruments under the fingertip rather than dialog parameters.
- **Steal for**: timeline editors and creative-tool playfulness.

### Darkroom — https://darkroom.co
Platform: iOS · Domain: photo editing · Recognition: Apple Design Awards 2020 winner
- **Mechanism**: library-native editing — scroll your whole Photos library with edits applied live in place; no import step, no project boundary, edits stay non-destructive.
- **Mechanism 2**: copy-paste edits across batches of photos; depth-aware and portrait-aware curves and filters.
- **Steal for**: in-flow editing surfaces that remove the "project" concept.

### Looom — https://www.iorama.studio
Platform: iOS (iPad) · Domain: animation · Recognition: Apple Design Awards 2020 winner
- **Mechanism**: time as spinning reels — animation layers are turntable-like spools (a music-mixer metaphor); you draw on a reel while it plays, so loop-making feels like playing an instrument.
- **Mechanism 2**: each "string" (layer) can spin at its own speed, making staggered loops trivial; scrub by physically spinning the reel.
- **Steal for**: playful timeline metaphors that teach themselves.

### Feather: Draw in 3D — https://www.feather.art
Platform: iOS (iPad) · Domain: 3D drawing · Recognition: Apple Design Awards 2025 winner (Visuals and Graphics)
- **Mechanism**: 3D with a 2D mental model — sketch a stroke from any angle and it lives in 3D space; orbit the view and keep drawing, blurring drawing and sculpting.
- **Mechanism 2**: Pencil-first, offline, one-time purchase — an expert domain (3D) reduced to a sketchbook's learning curve.
- **Steal for**: lowering the barrier of expert tools to finger-level simplicity.

## Travel, utility & information

### Flighty — https://flighty.com
Platform: iOS · Domain: travel / flight tracking · Recognition: Apple Design Awards 2023 winner (Interaction)
- **Mechanism**: Live Activity and Dynamic Island designed as airport signage — the compact island shows a departure countdown, expanded states echo split-flap departure boards, and gate/baggage/delay answers arrive by push before you ask.
- **Mechanism 2**: Passport — your flight history rendered as gorgeous, shareable custom artwork stats, turning a utility into a keepsake.
- **Steal for**: glanceable real-time status and lock-screen surfaces.

### Elk — https://elkapp.co
Platform: iOS · Domain: currency conversion · Recognition: Apple Design Award 2017 winner
- **Mechanism**: the rates table replaces the keypad — swipe left/right to scale the base amount in multiples (10×, 5×, 2×…), tap a row for exact precision; conversion becomes browsing, not typing.
- **Mechanism 2**: Apple Watch version driven entirely by the Digital Crown.
- **Steal for**: numeric input without keyboards.

### Sky Guide — https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sky-guide/id576588894
Platform: iOS · Domain: astronomy / AR · Recognition: Apple Design Award 2014 winner
- **Mechanism**: motion-sensing sky map — gyro and compass track where you point, labeling stars, constellations, planets and satellites in place, day or night.
- **Mechanism 2**: search that guides you physically — results draw directional arrows that lead you to pan the sky to the target object.
- **Steal for**: spatial/AR navigation patterns.

### Widgetsmith — https://widgetsmith.app
Platform: iOS · Domain: utility / customization · Recognition: 100M+ downloads; the iOS 14 home-screen personalization phenomenon
- **Mechanism**: the widget as a designed artifact — per-widget style, font, tint and photo customization with a live preview.
- **Mechanism 2**: time-based scheduling — different widgets for morning, afternoon and evening from the same slot; personalization as an emotional, visible payoff.
- **Steal for**: personalization and theming surfaces.

### Watch Duty — https://www.watchduty.org
Platform: iOS · Domain: civic safety / maps · Recognition: Apple Design Awards 2025 winner (Social Impact)
- **Mechanism**: map-first incident design — fire icons open a page with the burn perimeter drawn as a red outline, evacuation zones, wind conditions and live camera feeds, layered so the most urgent question ("am I safe?") is answered first.
- **Mechanism 2**: trust made visible — every update is credited to verified human reporters and official sources, with push alerts scoped to precise zones.
- **Steal for**: critical information design — calm, layered, trust-first.

### Arc Search — https://arc.net
Platform: iOS · Domain: browser / search · Recognition: Apple Design Awards 2024 finalist
- **Mechanism**: "Browse for Me" — a query becomes a single generated answer page with sources linked inline; attribution is part of the layout, not a footnote.
- **Mechanism 2**: keyboard-first search that jumps straight into results, and Pinch to Summarize to compress long pages.
- **Steal for**: AI answer surfaces that keep citations first-class.

### Mela — https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2025/
Platform: iOS · Domain: cooking / recipes · Recognition: Apple Design Awards 2025 finalist (Interaction)
- **Mechanism**: capture-to-structure — recipes are pulled from URLs or recognized from photos via the Vision framework and normalized into a clean structured format.
- **Mechanism 2**: cooking mode with Dynamic Island timers; ingredients push into Reminders integration.
- **Steal for**: turning messy real-world input into structured data.

### Lumy — https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2025/
Platform: iOS · Domain: photography utility / sun tracking · Recognition: Apple Watch App of the Year 2024; Apple Design Awards 2025 finalist
- **Mechanism**: golden hour as ambient data — sun and moon positions rendered as elegant arcs, then pushed to Home Screen widgets and Live Activities so the app works without opening.
- **Mechanism 2**: Watch-first design where a glance answers "how long until sunset?"
- **Steal for**: ambient information via widgets and Live Activities.

## Finance

### Copilot Money — https://copilot.money

- **Mechanism**: money dashboards with personality — custom category icons, animated spending charts, and monthly summaries written in plain language rather than tables.

## Health, fitness & accessibility

### Gentler Streak — https://gentlerstories.com/gentlerstreak
Platform: iOS · Domain: health & fitness · Recognition: Apple Design Awards winner (Social Impact) 2023 and again in 2024
- **Mechanism**: readiness-first coaching — the signature Streak band visualizes exertion vs. recovery, and "Go Gentler" proposes scaled-down sessions on tired days, top-of-watch, in empathetic copy ("A 20-min walk is perfect").
- **Mechanism 2**: a manual status switch for sickness, injury or mental-health days that pauses goals without breaking the streak — forgiving mechanics as design.
- **Steal for**: empathetic health tone and adaptive-goal systems.

### Slopes — https://getslopes.com
Platform: iOS · Domain: fitness / ski tracking · Recognition: Apple Design Awards 2022 winner (Interaction)
- **Mechanism**: 3D run replay — your day on the mountain traced onto resort maps you can rotate and scrub; lifts vs. runs auto-detected and separated.
- **Mechanism 2**: year-in-review storytelling that turns logged data into a shareable narrative.
- **Steal for**: turning tracked data into replayable stories.

### AnyDistance — https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2023/
Platform: iOS · Domain: fitness / social · Recognition: Apple Design Awards 2023 winner (Visuals and Graphics)
- **Mechanism**: activity as artifact — runs and rides become shareable posters with custom typography, gradient route maps and elevation-profile art; the share itself is the reward loop.
- **Steal for**: share-out design — make results beautiful enough to spread.

### SwingVision — https://swingvision.com
Platform: iOS · Domain: sports / tennis · Recognition: Apple Design Awards 2023 winner (Innovation)
- **Mechanism**: Apple Watch as line judge — an iPhone on a tripod auto-calls lines in real time while the Watch buzzes the verdict.
- **Mechanism 2**: automatic shot-traced video clips and stats overlaid on your own footage, hands-free.
- **Steal for**: passive capture plus instant officiating.

### Tiimo — https://tiimoapp.com
Platform: iOS · Domain: planning / accessibility · Recognition: iPhone App of the Year 2025; Apple Design Awards 2024 finalist
- **Mechanism**: the visual timeline — the day rendered as color-coded activity blocks with icons; blocks literally shrink as time passes, making time itself visible.
- **Mechanism 2**: full-screen color focus timer and icon-first scheduling designed for ADHD and autistic users; AI task breakdown lowers planning friction.
- **Steal for**: time visualization and accessibility-first planning.

### Be My Eyes — https://www.bemyeyes.com
Platform: iOS · Domain: accessibility · Recognition: Apple Design Awards 2021 winner (Social Impact)
- **Mechanism**: one tap connects a blind user to a sighted volunteer over live video — the entire UI is a single giant call button.
- **Mechanism 2**: Be My AI adds instant photo descriptions in natural language, with Specialized Help routing to company support lines.
- **Steal for**: ultra-low-friction help flows and human+AI hybrid assistance.

### Oko — https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2024/06/apple-announces-winners-of-the-2024-apple-design-awards/
Platform: iOS · Domain: accessibility / mobility · Recognition: Apple Design Awards 2024 winner (Inclusivity)
- **Mechanism**: the camera watches pedestrian signals and translates them into distinct haptic patterns — vibration tells a blind user which crosswalk signal is lit and exactly when it flips, no eyes or ears needed.
- **Mechanism 2**: directional haptic cues on Apple Watch guide orientation toward the crossing.
- **Steal for**: eyes-free interaction — haptics as a language.

### Loóna — https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2021/06/apple-announces-winners-of-the-2021-apple-design-awards/
Platform: iOS · Domain: sleep / wellbeing · Recognition: Apple Design Awards 2021 winner (Visuals and Graphics)
- **Mechanism**: bedtime moodscapes — guided coloring of calm landscapes fused with ambient sound and slow narration as a wind-down ritual.
- **Mechanism 2**: deliberately gentle interactions: no scores, no streaks, no urgency — the pacing itself is the feature.
- **Steal for**: calming interaction design and non-gamified engagement.

## Music & audio

### djay — https://www.algoriddim.com
Platform: iOS · Domain: music / DJ · Recognition: Apple Design Awards 2024 winner (Spatial Computing)
- **Mechanism**: skeuomorphic decks with real scratch physics — the turntable metaphor is fully playable, so pro DJing reads instantly on touch.
- **Mechanism 2**: Neural Mix faders — slide to mute vocals or drums of any track live, making an advanced DSP feat feel like moving a slider.
- **Steal for**: pro tools with physical, playable UI.

### Moises — https://moises.ai
Platform: iOS · Domain: music practice · Recognition: iPad App of the Year 2024; Apple Design Awards 2025 finalist
- **Mechanism**: any song splits into stems (vocals, drums, bass, other) via ML, each with its own volume/mute — studio power behind a simple player surface.
- **Mechanism 2**: per-track tempo and pitch change so practice adapts to the musician, not the reverse.
- **Steal for**: progressive complexity — a simple player hiding deep capability.

### StaffPad — https://staffpad.net
Platform: iOS (iPad) · Domain: music notation · Recognition: Apple Design Awards 2020 winner
- **Mechanism**: handwriting as structured input — write notation with the Pencil and it converts to engraved, publishable sheet music instantly, with orchestrated playback.
- **Steal for**: handwriting-to-data entry for any structured domain.

## Learning & delight

### Duolingo — https://www.duolingo.com
Platform: iOS · Domain: language learning · Recognition: Apple Design Awards 2023 winner (Delight and Fun)
- **Mechanism**: the 2022 path redesign — a nonlinear skill tree collapsed into a single linear path with guide characters, removing choice paralysis; streak mechanics (freeze, widget, XP quests) engineered into identity.
- **Mechanism 2**: the reactive mascot — Duo the owl watches, sulks and nags with contextual animations tied to your behavior, turning guilt into an interaction.
- **Steal for**: habit loops and character-driven motivation.

### CapWords — https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2025/
Platform: iOS · Domain: language learning · Recognition: Apple Design Awards 2025 winner (Delight and Fun)
- **Mechanism**: point the camera at an object and its foreign name becomes a sticker-like flashcard with real-world sound — learning is embedded in the physical room you're in.
- **Steal for**: camera-as-input learning loops.

### Bears Gratitude — https://apps.apple.com/ca/iphone/story/id1745165692
Platform: iOS · Domain: journaling / wellbeing · Recognition: Apple Design Awards 2024 winner (Delight and Fun)
- **Mechanism**: one gentle question per day, never a blank page — a hand-drawn bear companion accompanies each entry and reacts to it, lowering the emotional cost of starting.
- **Mechanism 2**: soft mood tracking and warm haptics tuned to feel like reassurance rather than feedback.
- **Steal for**: onboarding into journaling and low-pressure daily rituals.

### (Not Boring) Habits — https://notbor.ing
Platform: iOS · Domain: habits · Recognition: Apple Design Awards 2022 winner (Delight and Fun)
- **Mechanism**: a habit grid rebuilt as squishy physics tiles with "zingy haptics" on every touch — a boring utility category made tactile and alive.
- **Mechanism 2**: bold typographic screens that change daily, plus celebration particles as streaks grow; delight is the retention strategy.
- **Steal for**: making utility categories feel alive.

## Study these first

1. **Things 3** — the reference standard for interaction physics, quick capture, and list micro-interactions.
2. **Flighty** — the masterclass in glanceable surfaces: Live Activities, Dynamic Island, and push-first answers.
3. **Fantastical** — the definitive natural-language input pattern with live parse preview.
4. **Procreate** — the densest gesture system ever shipped on touch; progressive disclosure for experts.
5. **CARROT Weather** — personality plus a fully user-composable dashboard layout system.
